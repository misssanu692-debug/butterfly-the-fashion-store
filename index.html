About the Store

A local fashion destination at Ghadi Chowk, Balod.

Butterfly – The Fashion Store is a fashion and accessories store in Balod, Chhattisgarh, located infront of Sardar Patel Ground at Ghadi Chowk. The store is a convenient place for shoppers to explore fashion and accessories in person, with collections presented in a clean, welcoming and easy-to-browse setting.

Whether you are refreshing your everyday wardrobe or looking for something a little more special, our aim is simple — a pleasant shopping experience, close to home.
